# 📋 PRD - Blowmaggy: The Everything Issue

## Problem Statement
Modern e-commerce forces shoppers to visit multiple specialized stores for different needs — fashion on one site, groceries on another, electronics on yet another. This fragmentation creates a poor user experience: multiple accounts, fragmented carts, redundant checkouts, and inconsistent interfaces.

**Blowmaggy solves this**: One unified marketplace where customers can shop across 6 departments (Fashion, Beauty, Electronics, Home, Grocery, Kids) with a single cart, single checkout, and consistent experience.

## Personas

### 1. Priya — The Busy Mom (Primary)
- **Demographics**: 32, working mother, lives in urban India
- **Needs**: Buy kids' school supplies, household groceries, and personal care items in one trip
- **Pain point**: "I waste hours jumping between Amazon, Flipkart, and local stores"
- **Use case**: Adds groceries, kids' toys, and beauty products → one checkout

### 2. Rahul — The Tech Enthusiast
- **Demographics**: 26, software engineer, metro city
- **Needs**: Latest gadgets, accessories, and occasional fashion purchases
- **Pain point**: "I want tech deals but also need to buy birthday gifts"
- **Use case**: Browses electronics, adds headphones + gift for partner

### 3. Ananya — The Beauty Blogger
- **Demographics**: 24, content creator, style-conscious
- **Needs**: Trending beauty products, fashion items, photography equipment
- **Pain point**: "I follow trends everywhere but checkout is a mess"
- **Use case**: Curates beauty haul, fashion pieces, and lighting gear in one cart

### 4. Vikram — The Home Decorator
- **Demographics**: 35, newly married, setting up his first home
- **Needs**: Furniture, kitchenware, home decor, cleaning supplies
- **Pain point**: " furnishing my home takes 5 different websites"
- **Use case**: Bulk order of home essentials — all departments

## Scope

### In Scope (Phase 1 - MVP)
- ✅ 6 Departments: Fashion, Beauty, Electronics, Home, Grocery, Kids
- ✅ Product browsing with search & filters
- ✅ Shopping cart with persistent storage
- ✅ Multi-step checkout (Shipping → Payment → Review)
- ✅ Google Sign-In (simulated OAuth)
- ✅ Admin Dashboard (orders, analytics, departments)
- ✅ Responsive design (mobile-first)
- ✅ Promo code system
- ✅ Order tracking & confirmation

### Out of Scope (Future)
- Real payment gateway integration
- Multi-vendor marketplace
- Live inventory management
- AI recommendations engine
- Social features (reviews, wishlists sharing)
- Mobile app (native)
