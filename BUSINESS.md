# 💰 Business Model: Blowmaggy

## Revenue Streams

### 1. Direct Sales (Primary — 60%)
- Margin on product sales across 6 departments
- Target gross margin: 30-40% per department
- Higher margins in Electronics (25-35%) and Beauty (40-60%)
- Volume-driven in Grocery (15-25%) and Fashion (30-50%)

### 2. Commission Model (15%)
- Marketplace commissions from third-party sellers
- 10-15% commission on each sale
- Premium seller slots (featured placement): $500-2000/month
- Seller subscription tiers: Free / Pro ($99/mo) / Enterprise ($299/mo)

### 3. Advertising & Promotions (10%)
- Sponsored product listings (CPC model: $0.10-0.50/click)
- Banner ads from brands: $500-5000/month
- Email newsletter sponsorships: $1000-3000/campaign
- Homepage takeovers: $3000-10000/week

### 4. Subscription Service (5%)
- **Blowmaggy Prime**: $9.99/month
  - Free unlimited shipping
  - Early access to deals
  - Exclusive member-only products
  - Priority customer support
  - 5% cashback on all purchases

## Pricing Strategy
- **Penetration pricing** for market entry (15-25% below competitors)
- **Psychological pricing** ($9.99, $19.99, $29.99)
- **Dynamic pricing** for Electronics (competitive monitoring)
- **Bundle pricing** for cross-department purchases
- **Loss leaders** in Grocery to drive traffic

## Customer Acquisition
- **Digital marketing**: SEO, Google Ads, Social Media
- **Referral program**: Give $10, Get $10
- **Influencer partnerships**: Beauty & Fashion micro-influencers
- **Content marketing**: Blog, style guides, product reviews
- **Email marketing**: Abandoned cart recovery, personalized recommendations

## Unit Economics (Per Customer)
| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost) | <$15 |
| LTV (Lifetime Value) | >$200 |
| LTV:CAC Ratio | >13:1 |
| Monthly Active Users | >500K |
| Repeat Purchase Rate | >30% |
| Average Order Value | >$85 |

## Financial Projections (Year 1)
- **Revenue**: $2.4M (target)
- **Gross Margin**: 32%
- **Operating Expenses**: $1.8M
- **Net Loss** (Year 1): -$1.03M (investor-funded)
- **Break-even**: Month 14

## Competitive Advantage
1. **One-stop shopping** — 6 departments, 1 cart, 1 checkout
2. **Curated selection** — Not overwhelming, quality-checked products
3. **Local-first** — Same-day delivery in select cities
4. **Personalized experience** — Department-aware recommendations
5. **Community-driven** — Reviews, wishlists, sharing

## Funding Requirements
- **Seed Round**: $500K-1M
  - 40% Product & Engineering
  - 30% Marketing & Growth
  - 20% Operations & Logistics
  - 10% Legal & Admin

## Exit Strategy
- **Acquisition** by large player (Flipkart, Amazon, Reliance)
- **IPO** after 5 years, $50M+ revenue
- **Merger** with complementary marketplace
